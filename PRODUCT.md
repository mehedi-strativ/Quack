# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers using Quack as a native macOS menu-bar utility while actively working
in coding sessions. They glance at the notch between tasks and need useful
status without switching away from their editor or terminal.

## Product Purpose

Quack consolidates small macOS power tools into one quiet, always-available
companion. The notch agent surface makes active Codex, Claude Code, and opencode
sessions legible at a glance: what is working, what needs attention, and how
far the current work has progressed. Success means the user can decide whether
to stay in flow or intervene without opening another window.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, insightful. The interface should feel composed and precise,
with information prioritized by action rather than visual urgency. It can have
a small amount of Quack's playful character, but the developer's workflow is
the focus.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy monitoring dashboards, attention-seeking animations, and status
lights that cannot be expanded for explanation. Do not turn the
notch into a dense command center or require the user to open the panel to
understand whether an agent needs them.

## Design Principles

- Surface the next useful decision at a glance.
- Preserve developer flow: compact by default, detailed on hover.
- Make live state concrete with plain-language activity and progress.
- Use motion and color to clarify state, never to compete for attention.
- Keep the system quiet when nothing requires action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No additional project-specific requirements beyond standard readable text,
clear state distinctions, and reduced-motion support.
